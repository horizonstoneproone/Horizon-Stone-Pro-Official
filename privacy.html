Privacy Policy

How Horizon Stone Pro LLC collects, uses, and protects information from visitors to this website.

Last updated: September 2026

This policy explains what information Horizon Stone Pro LLC ("Horizon Stone Pro," "we," "us") collects through horizonstonepro.com, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using this site, you agree to the practices described here.

When you submit our contact form, we collect the information you provide: your name, email address, phone number, company name, the service you're requesting, and any project details you share with us.

When you browse the site, certain information is collected automatically through cookies and similar technologies, including your IP address, browser and device type, pages viewed, and referring website.

We use the following third-party tools to understand site traffic and improve our marketing. Each may set its own cookies or use similar tracking technology in your browser:

  • Google Analytics and Google Ads, to understand how visitors use the site and measure ad performance.
  • Meta Pixel, to measure the effectiveness of our Facebook and Instagram advertising.
  • Apollo.io website visitor tracking, to identify companies visiting our site for sales follow-up.

You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time. Doing so may limit some functionality of the site.

We use the information we collect to respond to quote requests, deliver the services you ask for, send invoices, follow up on inquiries, and improve our marketing and website performance.

We do not sell your information. We share it only with:

  • Service providers who help us operate the business, such as Stripe and QuickBooks for invoicing, and our contact form processor.
  • Subcontractor engineering firms or partners, but only the project information necessary to complete work you've requested, and only after an NDA if you've asked for one.
  • Government agencies, if required by law or as part of federal contracting compliance.

We retain project and contact information for as long as needed to deliver our services, meet legal and accounting requirements, and support any ongoing relationship. You can ask us to delete your information at any time by emailing us at the address below.

You can opt out of marketing emails using the unsubscribe link in any email we send, disable cookies through your browser, and request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information by contacting us.

This site is intended for business use and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect the information we collect. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.

Questions about this policy or your information can be sent to info@horizonstonepro.com or by mail to Horizon Stone Pro LLC, 3343 Peachtree Rd NE, Atlanta, GA 30326.
