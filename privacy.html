Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 24, 2026

Horizon Stone Pro LLC ("Horizon Stone Pro," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through www.horizonstonepro.com (the "Site"), how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

We collect information in two ways:

  • Information you provide directly — such as your name, email address, phone number, company, and project details when you submit our contact/request form.
  • Information collected automatically — such as your IP address, browser type, device information, pages viewed, and general location, collected through cookies and similar tracking technologies.

Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Like most websites, we use cookies and similar technologies to operate the Site and understand how visitors use it. Specifically, we use:

  • Google Analytics (GA4) — to understand site traffic and visitor behavior.
  • Google Ads — to measure the performance of our advertising campaigns.
  • Meta (Facebook) Pixel — to measure the performance of our social media advertising.
  • Apollo.io website visitor tracking — to help us identify and follow up with companies that visit our Site.

You can control cookies through your browser settings, including blocking or deleting them. Doing so may affect how parts of the Site function. You can also opt out of interest-based advertising through tools such as the Digital Advertising Alliance opt-out page or your browser's "Do Not Track" and ad-blocking settings.

How We Use Information

  • To respond to your inquiries and provide requested services
  • To operate, maintain, and improve the Site
  • To measure and improve our marketing and advertising
  • To communicate with you about your request or our services
  • To comply with legal obligations

Third-Party Services

We use trusted third-party providers to operate parts of the Site, including:

  • FormSubmit — processes and delivers messages submitted through our contact form.
  • Google (Analytics, Ads, Tag Manager) and Meta — provide analytics and advertising measurement.
  • Apollo.io — provides visitor identification for sales follow-up.

These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies. We do not sell your personal information.

Data Retention

We retain information you submit for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your request, maintain business records, and comply with legal obligations.

Your Choices

You may contact us at any time to ask what information we hold about you, to request a correction, or to request deletion, subject to legitimate business and legal record-keeping needs.

Children's Privacy

The Site is intended for business use and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Effective date" above reflects the latest revision.
