Full transparency

Who touches your project, in what order.

No subcontractor mystery, no ticket queue. Here's the actual structure behind every deliverable: the roles, the tools, and how your files are handled from the moment you send them.

Director of Operations

Owns the account relationship, confirms scope and pricing with you directly, and signs off before anything ships back.

Project manager

Your single point of contact. Breaks your request into tasks, assigns them internally, tracks the deadline, and answers your emails.

Estimating & takeoffs team

Reads the plan set, pulls quantities in Bluebeam, and builds the itemized cost sheet you'll receive.

CAD & drafting team

Handles redlines, as-builts, and detail drafting directly in AutoCAD or Revit, matched to your existing plan set.

Document control

Logs RFIs, transmittals, and change orders in Procore so the paper trail stays audit-ready.

Engineering team

Our licensed engineering team, covering all 50 states, handles the actual stamping and calculations behind every drawing that needs one.

Software stack

AutoCAD & Revit

Chain of custody

What happens to your files.

1

You send it

Upload through the form, or share a OneDrive, Google Drive, or Dropbox link. Nothing is opened by anyone outside the assigned team.

2

NDA, if you want one

We'll sign one before reviewing sensitive material, at no extra charge and no delay to your timeline.

3

Closed out on request

Files are handed off in your preferred format; backups are purged from OneDrive whenever you ask.
