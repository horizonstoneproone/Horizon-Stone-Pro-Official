Operational pipeline

How we support your project, step by step.

Each step names who does it, what tool it happens in, and what you get out of it, so there's no guessing where your request is at any given moment.

1

Tell us what you need

Fill out the form or call us. We'll ask a few questions to understand the scope before anything changes hands.

Handled by: Project manager
2

Scope review

We confirm exactly what's included, flag anything unclear, and send fixed pricing in writing before any work starts.

Handled by: Director of Operations
3

Core execution

Estimators, drafters, or our engineering team build your takeoffs, drawings, or document registers in the actual software listed below.

AutoCAD · Revit · Bluebeam · Procore
4

Scope-match check

Before anything ships, the Director of Operations checks the deliverable against the written scope from step 2.

If it's off scope, it's revised free
5

Direct delivery

Clean files in your requested format, sent straight to you or your project folder. No portal login required.

48–72h from scope confirmation
